Graduation Project — Team Project — Grade: A

Optimal Distribution of EV Charging Stations, Cairo

Cairo Governorate, Egypt

A spatial analysis of the optimal distribution of EV charging stations for cars and e‑motorcycles across Cairo Governorate. Used GIS multi‑criteria analysis (AHP and Weighted Overlay) in ArcGIS Pro to find suitable sites, then ran Kernel Density, Nearest Neighbor, Gap Analysis and Network Analyst Service Area analysis to study station distribution and road access. Slope, NDVI, NDBI and Land Surface Temperature — derived from SRTM, Sentinel‑2 and Landsat‑8 — were used to rule out unsuitable land, producing a final suitability map for underserved areas.

Coursework — Individual Project — Photogrammetry & Laser Scanning

LiDAR Point‑Cloud Processing & Building Extraction

Copenhagen area, Denmark

A photogrammetry and laser‑scanning exercise processing raw airborne LiDAR (.las) tiles into a classified, analysis‑ready point cloud. In CloudCompare, merged multiple flight‑line tiles, ran Statistical Outlier Removal (SOR) and noise filters to clean the data, cut out overhead cables and towers, then applied a Cloud‑to‑Surface (CSF) filter to separate ground from off‑ground returns and classify buildings and vegetation. Brought the classified LAS dataset into ArcGIS Pro to build elevation, RGB and intensity surfaces and derive a raster DEM/DSM of the study area.

Team Cartography Project

National Atlas of the Sultanate of Oman

Sultanate of Oman

A geographic‑historical atlas of Oman produced with a team of GIS students at the Faculty of Arts, Cairo University. Helped produce 25+ physical and human maps — terrain, geology, climate, water resources, population, education, services, infrastructure and tourism — covering all governorates. Used cartographic design and thematic mapping (choropleth and graduated‑symbol maps) in ArcGIS Pro to present geographic and demographic data.

NARSS Applied Study — July 2025

RUSLE Soil Erosion Assessment, North Sinai

North Sinai, Egypt

A RUSLE and GIS cloud‑based soil‑erosion assessment for North Sinai. Used Google Earth Engine, Sentinel‑2 imagery, CHIRPS rainfall data and NDVI to calculate the RUSLE model factors (R, K, LS, C, P) and classify soil‑loss severity.
